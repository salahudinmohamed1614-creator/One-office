Introduction

One Office – Many Services

ONE OFFICE is a private company with a public-service purpose. We help citizens, businesses and organizations prepare, submit, follow up and collect the services they need from the institutions of the Government of Somalia.

ONE OFFICE does not replace government institutions and does not make government decisions – we work as a professional service facilitator.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ONE OFFICE a government agency?
No. ONE OFFICE is a private company with a public-service purpose. It does not replace government institutions and does not make government decisions – it works as a professional service facilitator that helps you prepare, submit, follow up and collect your services.
How are fees calculated?
There are four types: Service Fee (ONE OFFICE's fee), Government Fee (the official government fee), Delivery Fee and Document Processing Fees. Government fees and ONE OFFICE fees are kept separate, and you receive proof of every payment you make.
How can I follow my request?
When you submit a request you receive a Tracking Number. On the “Track Request” page, enter the tracking number and your phone number to see the current status.
I live abroad – can you help me?
Yes. We serve Somalis living abroad who need services inside Somalia: certificates, court and civil documents, tax documents, business registration, government letters, Somali Visa and document delivery.
What do I need to get started?
Submit a request on the website or visit our office. Our team will tell you which documents are needed for the service you requested and the fees before any work starts.
Do you help new businesses or projects?
Yes. Our Business Support & Advisory team helps with Concept Notes, Company Profiles, Business Plans, SOPs, office and staff setup, all the way to the official launch.
